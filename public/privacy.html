Privacy notice

Updated 9 November 2026. This notice explains how the current CGPA Booster student project handles information.

Who runs this website

CGPA Booster is an independent student project run by Aryaman Gupta. For privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests, contact aryamangupta55@gmail.com. We may need to confirm account ownership before changing or deleting data.

What we store

When you register, we store your name, email, password hash, recovery-code hash and account dates. We also record successful logins and login counts. We store the information you choose to save, such as study progress, resume details, money records, posts, messages, profile details and uploads.

Who can see it

Published community cards, posts and replies are visible to other signed-in students. A shared portfolio can be opened by anyone with its link. Your private study, resume and money records are not displayed publicly.

Administrators can access account details, support requests, submitted attachments and moderation records through authorised tools. Chats covered by the chat moderation notice may be reviewed by administration to address abuse or misconduct. Read that notice before choosing to use chat. Do not submit passwords, bank credentials or identity documents.

Why we use it

We use this information to run your account, save your work, connect students, respond to support requests and protect the service from abuse. Emails are not currently verified and recovery uses your saved recovery code, rather than a password-reset email.

Hosting and external services

The website and database use Cloudflare. Its Turnstile security check processes browser and network information to help detect bots. YouTube and other external links open services with their own privacy rules. We do not currently embed YouTube videos or install advertising or third-party visitor analytics in the standard website.

Cookies and storage

A session cookie keeps you signed in. The website also uses browser storage for preferences. Read our cookie notice.

Retention and your choices

Saved records remain until removed through available controls or an account deletion request is handled. There is not currently an automatic account-retention schedule. Hosting backups may retain earlier copies for a limited provider-controlled period. Do not assume deleting a post removes copies already saved by others.

You can edit your profile, choose whether to share your portfolio and contact the owner about access, correction or deletion. Keep a copy of important work outside the website. Contact us if information in this notice does not match your experience.
