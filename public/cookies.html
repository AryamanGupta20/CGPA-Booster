Cookies and browser storage

Updated 11 November 2026.

What is used

ItemPurpose
campus_sessionKeeps your account signed in. It is an HTTP-only session cookie with a maximum lifetime of seven days. Logging out expires it.
Browser storageRemembers your light or dark appearance (cgpa-color-mode) and whether you dismissed the cookie notice (cgpa-cookie-notice). This is separate from your saved account data.
Cloudflare TurnstileChecks signup, login and recovery requests for bots. Cloudflare processes browser and network signals as part of this security check.

Your choices

The standard website does not currently use advertising cookies or optional visitor-tracking tools. There is no optional tracking to accept. Signing in requires the session cookie. You can log out or clear site data in your browser; clearing it may reset preferences and sign you out.

If optional tracking is added later, this notice and the relevant choices must be updated before it runs. External sites opened through resource links manage their own cookies.
